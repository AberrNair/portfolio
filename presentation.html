Problem & Rationale

Why Create a Non-Academic Portfolio?

Addressing the critical blind spots of traditional technical resumes

The Limitation of Standard Resumes

Flat, Unidimensional & Dry Text

  • ✕ Reduces multi-year national athletic rigor into a single bullet point line.
  • ✕ Fails to communicate pressure management, adrenaline control, and fast reflexes.
  • ✕ Leaves organizing leadership (Security Core, SIH 2025) without human context or visual evidence.
  • ✕ Completely ignores curiosity traits like world exploration and culinary experimentation.
  • ✕ Lacks emotional resonance—recruiters review hundreds of identical monochrome PDFs.
The Dedicated Platform Solution

Living Proof of Grit, Charisma & Craft

  • ✓ Authentic Visual Proof: Live match action, trophy presentation, and podium addresses.
  • ✓ Cross-Functional Transferability: Shows how athletic stamina directly powers 36-hour hackathons.
  • ✓ Interactive Engagement: Switchable views, responsive filters, and dynamic timelines.
  • ✓ Memorable Personal Brand: The "Champion's Realm" aesthetic creates an unforgettable impression.
  • ✓ Global Accessibility: Deployed live on GitHub, Netlify, and Vercel for worldwide access.
UX Architecture

Heuristic Principles Applied (Part 1)

Implementing Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics for seamless interaction

H01

Visibility of System Status

Keeping the user informed at all times

  • • Sticky Glassmorphic Nav: Dynamically highlights active section as user scrolls.
  • • Live Status Indicators: Pulsing red broadcast dots ("Tournament Action", "Active 2026").
  • • Interactive Matrix Feedback: Real-time competency detail breakdown on click.
Immediate Visual Feedback
H02

Match: System & Real World

Speaking user language through real-world metaphors

  • • Tournament Timeline: Natural chronological progression (School Nationals ➜ West Zone ➜ Yuva Gold).
  • • Life Metaphors: "The Arena" for sports, "The Forge" for fitness, "The Hearth" for cooking.
  • • Conversational Phrasing: Human storytelling rather than developer jargon.
Intuitive Conceptual Models
H03

User Control & Freedom

Flexible viewing options and effortless resets

  • • Athlete Photo Switcher: Instant 4-tab control ([Trophy], [Match], [Gym], [Travel]).
  • • Jump-to-Section Links: Smooth-scroll anchors leap across sections without page reloads.
  • • Return to Top Shortcut: Persistent footer controls restore viewport instantly.
Total Visitor Autonomy
UX Architecture

Heuristic Principles Applied (Part 2)

Consistency, cognitive load reduction & minimalist visual hierarchy

H04

Consistency & Standards

Strict design token uniformity

  • • Unified Design Tokens: Universal card radius (16px), gold borders, and obsidian styling.
  • • Symmetrical Card Pairs: Mirrored structures in Fitness ("Gym" & "Core") and People ("Connecting" & "Speaking").
  • • Standardized Badge Taxonomy: Distinct color-coded pills for roles and tournaments.
Familiar & Predictable Flow
H06

Recognition Over Recall

Visual scannability & scannable data

  • • 4 Hero Stat Cards: Instant scannable takeaways (National, Zonal, Winner, 3 Events).
  • • Visual Skills Heatmap: Explicit grid connecting pursuits to professional competencies.
  • • Categorical Photo Labels: Clear tags eliminate guessing ("Heritage Wonder", "Public Speaking").
Low Cognitive Burden
H08

Aesthetic & Minimalist Design

High signal-to-noise ratio

  • • Obsidian Dark Palette: Minimizes screen glare while amplifying photography contrast.
  • • Curated White Space: Balanced margins give breathing room to achievements without clutter.
  • • Subtle Micro-Interactions: Tactile hover lifts (-6px) instead of disruptive jarring animations.
Clean Content Priority
Visual Identity

Design System: 'The Champion's Realm'

Cinematic Game of Thrones Obsidian Aesthetic built on modern React architecture

Color Palette & Atmosphere

Deep Obsidian (#080B11) and Royal Midnight (#0D131F) provide an imposing foundation. Dragonfire Gold (#F59E0B) and Bright Amber symbolize victory and regal poise, with Crimson (#DC2626) accents for athletic intensity.

Typography System

Headings powered by Cinzel & Cinzel Decorative evoke ancient stone inscriptions and championship titles. Body text rendered in clean, legible Inter & Outfit guarantees effortless reading across mobile and desktop displays.

Motion & Micro-Interactions

Subtle canvas floating ember sparks, smooth spring card hover elevations (-6px translateY), animated numeric counter tickers on the Hero stats, and smooth-scroll navigation anchors create a tactile, premium feel.

Tech Stack & Performance

Built with React 19, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS 4, and Framer Motion. Zero UI kit bloat. Lightning-fast build times (2.2s), mobile-first responsive breakpoints, and pre-configured deployment rules for Netlify and Vercel.
